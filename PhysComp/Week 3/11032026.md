# Expressive Mechanics Brainstorming

My impression of computer vision has been ruined by elephants. I hear "computer vision" and my brain automatically defaults to dancing elephants...

So, to counter this unfortunate mental model, I aim to create a very cool computer vision project with this one.

Requirements/suggestions:
- Kinetic sculpture (mechanical movement)
- Electro-mechanical movement
- Microcontroller + computer vision

Make sure to hide all wires, creating proper housing/enclosure.

I'm largely inspired by perpetual machines, less so of the perpetual movement (I know, kinda defeats the purpose) but moreso the free-fall and gravitational movements of components within the perpetual machines (think spheres, levers/flaps, etc.) are very satisfying.
