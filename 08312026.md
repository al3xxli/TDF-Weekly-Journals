Three paragraphs of goals at MDes:

My primary goal in the MDes program is to transition from architecture into physical product and industrial design. I want to build on the spatial, material, and design thinking skills I developed through architecture while learning how to design products that are more direct, tangible, and user-centered. I hope to develop a stronger understanding of the full product design process, from research and ideation through prototyping, refinement, and production.

I also want to use the MDes as an opportunity to explore the breadth of opportunities within consumer design. Rather than committing immediately to a single industry, I want to experiment across areas such as consumer electronics, sports and lifestyle products, furniture, and other physical products. This exploration will help me identify where my architectural background and emerging product design skills can create the most value, while also building a portfolio that demonstrates versatility and a strong physical design sensibility.

Finally, I want to deepen my understanding of the relationship between physical products, technology, and human interaction. In particular, I am interested in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and Tangible User Interfaces (TUI), and how physical form can become an integral part of an interactive experience. I hope to explore how materials, form, tactility, and embedded technology can work together to create more intuitive and engaging products, ultimately developing a design practice that bridges physical design, interaction, and emerging technology.

My superpower is physical design, particularly understanding how form, ergonomics, and human interaction come together. I see this sensitivity to physical experience as a strength I can bring into product design, especially when designing objects that need to balance usability, comfort, and visual character.
