Class Journal:

This was the first intro class with TJ, we started off with a small exercise of word-by-word prediction for a sentence "once upon a time..." which led to the introduction of how LLMs work with probability.

Something I learned was that the model does not adapt to responses, contrary to what I had believed about reinforcement models and learning. The reinforcement could be part of a larger pipeline when training, but the actual model will always refer to the information it was initially trained on.

We then progressed into a temperature visualizer, which was very helpful as I only previously understood it as a "level of creativity" but didn't know how exactly that "creativity" was distributed. We then went through the application layers of consumer LLMs, which I already knew about.

To end off the class, we input our responses from [the previous class](08312026) into a [LLM interface](https://agentstudio.aroughidea.com/w/computational-design-ai-workshop-series-2026) that TJ coded.
